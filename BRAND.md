# Laal — Brand Voice & Copy Glossary

**Laal (لال)** = precious one / beloved child / jewel. Belief: *nobody is a "case"
or a "burden" — everyone is someone's Laal.* Domain: **laal.app**.

**Promise:** Every life is precious. (alts: "Everyone is someone's Laal.",
"Protect someone precious.", "Small kindness. Life-changing impact.")

**Voice:** human, warm, trustworthy, humble — like a caring family member helping
quietly. Never corporate, never a pity campaign, never a fundraising machine.

**North star:** *Would we say this if the person needing help was our own family
member?* If yes, publish. If no, rewrite.

## Word swaps (apply to all user-facing copy)

| Don't say | Say |
|---|---|
| Donate (CTA) | **Help Now**, Support, Give Support |
| Donation (noun) | support, contribution, act of kindness |
| Donor / user who gives | **Supporter**, Helper, Community |
| Cases | **Stories**, People, Needs |
| Beneficiaries | **People We Support**, Families, Community Members |
| Help the poor / needy / unfortunate | Support a family, Stand with someone |
| Save / rescue the helpless | Help someone rebuild, Give hope, Protect dignity |
| Applicant verified / Case approved | **Story reviewed**, **Need verified** |
| "You received charity" | "Your community supported you", "You matter" |

Recipients are **People / Families / Laals** — never poor, victims, or cases.

## Standard copy

- **Hero (chosen):** "Everyone is someone's Laal." — body: "A mother. A father. A
  child. A dream. Behind every request is a person who matters." — button:
  "Support Someone Today".
- **Trust:** "Every story on Laal is reviewed before receiving support." /
  "We verify needs so kindness reaches the right people."
- **Donation amounts (capped at $50 for launch):**
  - $10 — "Provide a moment of relief"
  - $25 — "Create meaningful support"
  - $50 — "Make a lasting difference"
- **Confirmation:** "Thank you for protecting someone precious." / "You helped
  remind someone they are not alone."
- **Empty — no stories:** "New stories are being reviewed. Check back soon to
  support someone precious."
- **Empty — no support yet:** "Your first act of kindness starts here."
- **Notifications:** prefer "You just made someone's day brighter." over
  "Donation successful"; "Together, supporters changed this story." over "goal
  reached".

> Note: internal/technical terms in code, admin tooling, and the database
> (campaign, beneficiary, donation) stay as-is — this
> glossary governs **user-facing copy**.
